# Competitor Sources — Initial Research

Reviewed for an initial evidence pass on 2026-11-20. These links are source pointers; research coverage varies by competitor and page. The references are predominantly official sources, but some are global, training, accreditation, or contextual pages rather than local Indonesian service catalogues.


## 1. SGS
- Indonesia certification services: https://www.sgs.com/en-id/our-services/certification-and-audit/assessment-auditing-and-certification — explicitly lists ISO 13485, 14001, 22301, 45001, 50001, 9001, 27001, 27701, plus other schemes.
- SGS Indonesia training: https://www.sgs.com/en-id/our-services/training — course catalogue includes ISO 22000, 20000-1 and others; training mention is not treated as proof of certification offer.
- SGS Australia certification catalogue: https://www.sgs.com/en-au/our-services/certification-and-audit/assessment-auditing-and-certification — includes ISO 22000 in a country-specific catalogue; use only as global/group contextual evidence.
- SGS UK certification process standards page: https://www.sgs.com/en-gb/our-company/about-sgs/sgs-in-united-kingdom/your-certification-process-explained — mentions ISO 20000-1 and additional standards in UK context.

## 2. Bureau Veritas
- General certification services: https://group.bureauveritas.com/services-solutions/certification-services — overview of certification and QHSE/management system topics.
- Bureau Veritas Cybersecurity licences/standards: https://cybersecurity.bureauveritas.com/our-licences-to-operate-certificates — ISO 9001, 14001, 17025, 27001, 45001 in a cybersecurity entity's own licence/certificate context, not a full Indonesia service list.
- Competence/job posting: search result for Auditor/Trainer includes ISO 37001 among qualifications; treated as context only, not catalogue evidence.

## 3. TÜV Rheinland
- Indonesia management system certification: https://certification.tuv.com/id-id/our-services/sertifikasi-sistem-manajemen — core examples include ISO 9001, 14001, 27001, 45001, 50001.
- Global management system certification: https://certification.tuv.com/gb-en/services/management-system-certification — general portfolio and combined certification context.
- Global certification/auditing page: https://certification.tuv.com/it-en — lists ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 42001.
- Accreditation mark policy document (North America scope): https://www.tuv.com/content-media-files/usa/pdfs/imprint-corporate-procurement/2025/ms-0005743-promoting-certification-and-using-the-trna-and-anab-marks.pdf — contains ISO 27701 and ISO 42001 in scope; not Indonesia-specific evidence.

## 4. TÜV NORD
- Indonesia recognitions and accreditations: https://www.tuv-nord.com/id/en/company/our-recognitions-accreditations/ — official Indonesia page explicitly lists KAN accreditation identifiers for ISO 9001 (LSSM-016-IDN), ISO 14001 (LSSML-008-IDN), ISO 45001 (LSSMK3-003-IDN), ISO/IEC 27001 (LSSMKI-001-IDN), ISO 37001 (LSSMAP-003-IDN), ISO 21001, ISO 50001 (LSSME-001-IDN), ISO 13485 (LSSMAK-001-IDN), and ISO 22000/HACCP (LSSMKP-004-IDN / LSSHACCP-011-IDN). This is stronger local accreditation evidence than a general global portfolio. The page does not list ISO 22301, ISO/IEC 20000-1, ISO/IEC 27701 or ISO/IEC 42001.

## 5. SUCOFINDO
- Indonesia certification services overview: https://www.sucofindo.co.id/id/layanan/sertifikasi — general service categories.
- Indonesia ISO management system service page: https://www.sucofindo.co.id/id/layanan/layanan-terdiversifikasi/sertifikasi-sistem-manajemen-iso — page discusses management system certification and mentions ISO 27001, ISO 9001 and food safety management in related products.
- English management system service page: https://www.sucofindo.co.id/en/services/iso-management-system-certification — contextual reference.
- Certification brochure PDF: https://www.sucofindo.co.id/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Certification-Brochure-mei-2022.pdf — older brochure; not assumed to reflect the current full portfolio.

## 6. MUTU International
- MUTU profile (official): https://mutucertification.com/profile/about-mutu-international/ — history states KAN accreditation as ISO 9001 certification body LSSM-008-IDN; company profile also describes its ISO 45001 portfolio history.
- INSPECT partnership service page: https://mutucertification.com/inspect-certification/ — explicitly says MUTU cooperates with an IAS-accredited Turkish certification body and can help clients obtain ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001 and ISO 13485 through INSPECT. Record these as partner-delivered offers, not as MUTU's own KAN-accredited scope.
- MUTU's ISO management-system certification rules (PDF): https://mutucertification.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/MUTU-5001-ABPI-3-6-Aturan-Pelaksanan-Sertifikasi-9001-14001-37001-27001.pdf — identifies KAN accreditation IDs for ISO 9001 (LSSM-008-IDN), ISO 14001 (LSSML-002-IDN), ISO 37001 (LSSMAP-004-IDN), and ISO/IEC 27001 (LSSMKI-010-IDN). The PDF revision is dated 2021; verify current register before treating it as current scope.

- BSI Indonesia certificate directory — ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 client profile (PT Trakindo Utama): https://www.bsigroup.com/en-ID/products-and-services/assessment-and-certification/validation-and-verification/client-directory-profile/PT_TRA-0047764320-035/ — records issued certificates for the three standards.
- BSI Indonesia certificate directory — further ISO/IEC 27001 client examples: https://www.bsigroup.com/en-ID/products-and-services/assessment-and-certification/validation-and-verification/client-directory-certificate/IS%20791674/ and https://www.bsigroup.com/en-ID/products-and-services/assessment-and-certification/validation-and-verification/client-directory-certificate/IS%20774023/.
- Indonesia training schedule PDF: https://www.bsigroup.com/globalassets/localfiles/en-id/Training%20Schedule/2024/bsi-indonesia-training-schedule-2024.pdf — standards/topic coverage includes 22301, 37001, 27701, 20000-1 and 50001; training is contextual only, not proof of certification offer.

## 8. DQS
- Indonesia/global accreditation overview: https://www.dqsglobal.com/id/tentang/akreditasi-dan-integritas/akreditasi-dan-notifikasi/akreditasi-dan-notifikasi-dqs — says DQS evaluates against 100+ standards; accreditation table lists a wide portfolio globally including ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, 50001, 27001, 20000-1, 22301, 22000, 37001, 13485, and others. This is global accreditation context; Indonesian KAN scope on this page is narrower.
- Knowledge page: https://www.dqsglobal.com/id/wawasan/pusat-pengetahuan-dqs/apa-itu-sertifikasi — overview of commonly used standards, not necessarily a complete offer.

## 9. URS Indonesia
- URS global accreditation (Turkey): https://www.urs-turkey.com/accreditations — global group accreditation information, including ISO 9001, 14001, 20000, 27001, 22000, 13485 and other schemes.
- URS Malaysia accreditation page: https://www.ursmalaysia.com/accreditations — global scheme list including 45001.
- URS Indonesia LinkedIn profile: https://id.linkedin.com/company/urs-id — local company profile and service standard list; third-party social page and not an official technical scope document.

## 10. TQCSI Indonesia
- Official certification standards page: https://www.tqcsi.com/services/certification-of-standards — list of JASANZ accredited programs (ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, 55001, 27001, 22000, 13485, 37001) and other programs (including ISO 22301, 20000-1, 50001, ISO/IEC 42001). Group/global evidence; local Indonesian scope should be verified.
- TQCSI Indonesia systems policies: https://www.tqcsi.id/systems-policies — standards policy list including ISO 22000, 9001, 14001, 13485, 45001, 27001, 37001; not a portfolio/authorization document by itself.

## 11. IAS Indonesia
- Indonesia service page: https://ias-indonesia.com/ — explicitly lists ISO 9001, 14001, 22000, GMP, 27001, HACCP, 20000, 45001.
- Indonesia system certification page: https://ias-indonesia.org/sertifikasi-sistem/ — lists ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, 22000, 27001, 50001, IATF 16949.
- IAS accreditation page: https://iascertification.com/accreditation/ — broader accreditation/training routes; references lead auditor courses such as ISO 22301 and ISO 13485. Treat course mention as contextual evidence, not certification service evidence.

## Source-quality note
