# Competitor Sources — Initial Research

Reviewed for an initial evidence pass on 2026-11-20. These links are source pointers; research coverage varies by competitor and page. The references are predominantly official sources, but some are global, training, accreditation, or contextual pages rather than local Indonesian service catalogues.


## 1. SGS
- Indonesia certification services: https://www.sgs.com/en-id/our-services/certification-and-audit/assessment-auditing-and-certification — explicitly lists ISO 13485, 14001, 22301, 45001, 50001, 9001, 27001, 27701, plus other schemes.
- SGS Indonesia training: https://www.sgs.com/en-id/our-services/training — course catalogue includes ISO 22000, 20000-1 and others; training mention is not treated as proof of certification offer.
- SGS Australia certification catalogue: https://www.sgs.com/en-au/our-services/certification-and-audit/assessment-auditing-and-certification — includes ISO 22000 in a country-specific catalogue; use only as global/group contextual evidence.
- SGS UK certification process standards page: https://www.sgs.com/en-gb/our-company/about-sgs/sgs-in-united-kingdom/your-certification-process-explained — mentions ISO 20000-1 and additional standards in UK context.

## 2. Bureau Veritas

- Bureau Veritas Cybersecurity licences/standards: https://cybersecurity.bureauveritas.com/our-licences-to-operate-certificates — ISO 9001, 14001, 17025, 27001, 45001 in a cybersecurity entity's own licence/certificate context, not a full Indonesia service list.
