# ISO Competitor Service Matrix Research

## Purpose
Build a source-backed comparison of the ISO management-system service catalogues of the 11 previously selected reference companies. This is competitor research, not a claim about TNCV's accreditation, authorization, or current delivery capability.


## Candidate standards (12)
- ISO 9001 — Quality management
- ISO 14001 — Environmental management
- ISO 45001 — Occupational health and safety
- ISO 22000 — Food safety management
- ISO/IEC 27001 — Information security management
- ISO 37001 — Anti-bribery management
- ISO 50001 — Energy management
- ISO 22301 — Business continuity management
- ISO 13485 — Medical devices quality management
- ISO/IEC 20000-1 — IT service management
- ISO/IEC 27701 — Privacy information management
- ISO/IEC 42001 — AI management system

These 12 standards are a comparison shortlist, not an approved TNCV service catalogue. ISO/IEC 27701 and ISO/IEC 42001 should be checked for current editions, scope, accreditation and scheme eligibility before describing certification offers.


## Status legend
- **Listed (L):** standard is explicitly named in the official source reviewed.
- **Not found on page (NF):** not explicitly found in the reviewed official page(s); this does not prove the competitor does not offer it elsewhere.
- **Not checked (NC):** no adequate official source reviewed yet.
- **Context only (C):** source mentions a related scheme/training/competence, not evidence of certification offer.

Avoid interpreting search snippets as complete catalogues. Prefer current official service pages, and preserve the page URL and date reviewed in the source notes. When a page is global or a different country page, mark it as global/other-market evidence rather than Indonesia-specific proof.

## Research workflow
1. Review official service pages for each company.
2. Record exact ISO standard identifiers explicitly shown.
3. Mark the matrix **L** only when the source explicitly lists the standard.
4. Distinguish certification from training, consultancy, auditing, validation, verification, and internal accreditation.
5. Record country context (Indonesia, global, or another country) and date of review.
6. Recheck gaps before drawing market-share or competitor-count conclusions.
7. Use findings to inform content architecture and SEO topics, not to imply that TNCV is authorized to issue certificates.
