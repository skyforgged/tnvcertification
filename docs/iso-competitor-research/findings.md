# Preliminary Findings and Recommendations

**Research date:** 2026-11-20  
**Branch:** `research/iso-competitor-service-matrix`  
**Scope:** 11 competitor names from the prior benchmark and a shortlist of 12 standards.


## What the initial evidence shows

The four broad/core standards (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, and ISO/IEC 27001) appear in most of the official sources reviewed. Food safety ISO 22000, energy ISO 50001, medical-device ISO 13485, IT service management ISO/IEC 20000-1, and business continuity ISO 22301 also recur among larger/global portfolios and specialist pages. The evidence for ISO/IEC 27701 and ISO/IEC 42001 is more limited or specific to global programs in this pass.

## Competitive positioning recommendation

1. **Build a strong core catalogue first:** ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO/IEC 27001. These are highly recurrent and should have the strongest content depth and clear conversion paths.
2. **Expand next into frequent specialist standards:** ISO 22000, ISO 50001, ISO 13485, ISO/IEC 20000-1, ISO 22301, and ISO 37001. Their suitability depends on confirmed TNCV service authorization, scope, partner, and competent personnel.
3. **Treat privacy and AI as emerging topics, not quick certification claims:** ISO/IEC 27701 and ISO/IEC 42001 need careful checks of the scheme, accreditation, authorization and edition before a service is advertised.
4. **Add adjacent non-ISO schemes only as separate taxonomy entries:** e.g., HACCP, FSSC 22000, IATF 16949, SMK3, product certification, sustainability and verification. Avoid mislabeling them as ISO certification services.
5. **Prioritize quality of pages over catalogue size:** each published service should explain scope, target sectors, readiness, audit/certification stages, FAQ, related content and contact CTA. Build reusable content templates and structured data, but never claim accredited scope without evidence.


## Recommended first content batch (proposed only)

- 6 primary ISO pages: 9001, 14001, 45001, 22000, 27001, 37001.
- Follow-up expansion after scope confirmation: 50001, 22301, 13485, 20000-1.
- Specialist/emerging pages after scheme and authority checks: 27701 and 42001.
- CSMS Contractor Safety Management System stays separate from the ISO catalogue and in draft pending verification of scheme criteria, scope, process, authority, and recognition. It is not the automotive cybersecurity CSMS under UN R155.


## Limitations and next actions

- No fresh repository build or local content validation was run as part of this research branch.
- Official pages may be global, country-specific, training-focused, accreditation-focused, or incomplete.
- No conclusion is drawn that an unlisted standard is not offered by a competitor.
- The next research phase should verify every matrix cell through the official Indonesia service catalogue and/or accreditation scope, then update status, URL, page context, and reviewed date.
- TNCV’s own certification scope/authority has not been validated in this research. The 12 standards are a content planning shortlist only.
