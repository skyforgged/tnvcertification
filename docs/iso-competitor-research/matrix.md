## Initial takeaways (not market-share claims)
- The core quartet ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO/IEC 27001 appears repeatedly in the reviewed sources.
- Food safety ISO 22000, energy ISO 50001, business continuity ISO 22301, and medical-device ISO 13485 are also present in many of the larger/global portfolios and specialist catalogues.
- Specialized standards (ISO 27701 and ISO 42001) are less consistently shown across the reviewed pages; the evidence suggests possible differentiation topics but not that they are universally absent.
- TNCV should not use this preliminary competitor matrix as proof of its own capability, authorization, or accreditation scope.
