**Reference:** TNCV-CSMS-ORG-001 (proposed)

## 1. Purpose and boundaries

Define the functions, segregation controls and resource evidence needed to operate the proposed CSMS Readiness Review & Documentation Support service safely.

Role titles in this document are proposed functions, not evidence that a person has been appointed, is competent, or holds statutory, accreditation, principal or scheme-owner authorization. One person may cover more than one function only after a documented competence, capacity and impartiality review confirms that the combination is acceptable.

This framework does not authorize certification, principal qualification, official scoring or vendor approval.

## 3. Role segregation and incompatibility controls

1. The person who prepares or materially changes a client's evidence must not independently review that same work as an impartial readiness reviewer where this creates a conflict.
2. An assessor must not approve their own report as the sole technical reviewer.
3. A technical reviewer must not review work outside their demonstrated competence.
4. A complaint must be investigated by a person not responsible for the challenged activity wherever practicable. If this is not possible, an external or otherwise independent reviewer should be appointed.
5. The impartiality reviewer must have authority to require safeguards, restrict scope, reassign personnel or decline the engagement.
6. The engagement approver confirms that source, competence, impartiality, contract, privacy and resource gates are complete; the approver does not replace technical review.
7. Where a small team makes separation impracticable, document the threat, independent safeguard and rationale. If the threat cannot be reduced to an acceptable level, do not accept or continue the work.
8. Disclosure alone does not eliminate a conflict.

## 5. Assignment authorization checklist

Complete one record for every engagement before work begins:

- [ ] Engagement ID, client legal entity, principal/project, sector and work scope recorded.
- [ ] Applicable principal source pack identified, version checked and independently verified.
- [ ] Each assigned person's competence evidence reviewed against the exact tasks and scope.
- [ ] Mandatory statutory, client, principal or scheme-owner credentials independently verified where applicable.
- [ ] Any competence limitation, supervision requirement or scope restriction documented.
- [ ] Conflict declarations collected and reviewed; safeguards approved and effective.
- [ ] Assessor, technical reviewer, report approver and complaint route assigned with appropriate separation.
- [ ] Workload, availability, backup coverage and delivery time are realistic.
- [ ] Site access, induction, PPE and travel controls assessed if site work is included.
- [ ] Customer information handling, secure transfer, storage and access permissions approved.
- [ ] Engagement acceptance and resource approval recorded by the authorized role.
- [ ] No work starts until all mandatory items are closed or an explicitly authorized, justified exception is documented; no exception may override hard-stop rules.

## 8. Escalation and review

Escalate to management before acceptance or continuation where:
- A required role is vacant or lacks delegated authority.
- Competence or mandatory credentials cannot be demonstrated.
- Conflict safeguards are ineffective or independence cannot be maintained.
- Source authenticity, revision or applicability remains uncertain.
- Secure handling or lawful processing of customer data cannot be demonstrated.
- Workload, timing, budget or backup resources are inadequate.
- A complaint, serious error, data incident or material scope change affects service integrity.

Review this plan before the first pilot, after a material change, and at the management review interval once approved.

## 10. Impartiality governance and decision authority (RA-06)

The impartiality / conflict reviewer must be appointed with documented authority to:
- Require declarations from employees, management, subcontractors and external experts before assignment.
- Obtain relevant engagement, relationship and commercial information needed for screening.
- Evaluate actual, potential and perceived threats independently of sales targets and delivery pressure.
- Require safeguards, reassignment, scope restrictions, pause, rejection or termination.
- Escalate attempted influence, concealment or retaliation directly to the designated management authority.
- Verify implementation and effectiveness evidence before authorizing a proceed decision.

### Minimum segregation

| Decision / activity | Required separation |
|---|---|
| Conflict declaration | Made by each assigned person and relevant external resource; not completed solely by the service owner on their behalf |
| Initial screening | Coordinated by service owner; facts and declarations retained |
| Threat evaluation | Performed by appointed impartiality reviewer who is not commercially accountable for winning the engagement |
| High-risk / unacceptable decision | Escalated to independent management authority; unacceptable hard-stop cannot be overridden for commercial reasons |
| Safeguard implementation | Assigned to named owner other than the impartiality approver where practicable |
| Safeguard effectiveness check | Verified by impartiality reviewer or another competent independent person |
| Final proceed approval | Recorded by delegated approver after independent impartiality clearance and all other engagement gates |
| Conflict-related complaint | Investigated by a person independent of the challenged work; external support used if internal independence is not practicable |

A client disclosure or consent does not replace effective safeguards. If no competent and independent reviewer is available, or a hard-stop conflict cannot be removed, the engagement must not proceed.

### Engagement-level conflict gate

- [ ] Declarations collected from all assigned personnel and external resources.
- [ ] Prior advisory, training, implementation and document-preparation services checked for the same client and scope.
- [ ] Personal, financial, ownership, employment, referral and commercial interests assessed.
- [ ] Fee terms checked for success-based or outcome-linked incentives.
- [ ] Confidentiality, competing duties and information access evaluated.
- [ ] Threat level, rationale and specific safeguards recorded.
- [ ] Safeguards implemented and effectiveness evidence reviewed.
- [ ] Independent reviewer and delegated approver identified.
- [ ] Proceed / restricted / pause / decline decision recorded before work starts.
- [ ] Reassessment triggers and responsible contact identified.

Use the [Impartiality & Conflict of Interest Register](impartiality-conflict-register.md) as the controlled engagement record. No engagement may start while a material conflict field, safeguard or required approval remains pending.


## 11. Technical review and report-release governance (RA-07)

The technical reviewer must be appointed for the specific sector, principal criteria, CSMS subject matter, assessment method and report scope. Before assignment, the service owner must verify the reviewer's current competence evidence, authorization scope, availability and engagement-specific conflict clearance. A course certificate or job title alone is not sufficient evidence of competence.

### Required decision separation

| Activity / decision | Minimum accountable role | Separation / evidence control |
|---|---|---|
| Perform assessment and draft report | Assessor / report author | Records objective evidence and identifies all contributors |
| Technical review | Appointed technical reviewer | Must not have performed or materially authored the work under review; independent competence and conflict clearance recorded |
| Correct review comments | Author / assessor | Responds with evidence; preserves original record and revision history |
| Resolve technical disagreement | Independent technical authority / senior reviewer | Competent for disputed scope, not involved in disputed work, with decision rationale retained |
| Confirm impartiality and conflicts | Appointed impartiality reviewer | Separate from sales/delivery pressure; clearance remains valid through review and release |
| Authorize report release | Delegated report approver | Separate, documented release authority; verifies review outcome and exact report version |
| Control review records | Document controller / designated record owner | Retains checklist, comment log, escalation, approval and distribution evidence |

### Technical reviewer authority

The reviewer is authorized, within the approved delegation, to request source/evidence clarification, require correction or rework, reject unsupported findings or conclusions, place the report on hold, and escalate unresolved disagreement. The reviewer cannot alter evidence history, silently change assessor conclusions, waive impartiality hard stops, or authorize release unless separately delegated as report approver with appropriate separation.

### Minimum reviewer competence and appointment record

- [ ] Named individual, role, appointment date and approving authority.
- [ ] Defined review scope, criteria, sector and permitted report types.
- [ ] Verified relevant qualifications/credentials and experience.
- [ ] Demonstrated competence in evidence evaluation, traceability, sampling, findings and report review.
- [ ] Observed review/work-sample assessment and evaluator decision where required.
- [ ] Current authorization, restrictions, supervision and reassessment date.
- [ ] Engagement-specific conflict declaration and independence decision.
- [ ] Capacity and availability confirmation.
- [ ] Signed acceptance of review responsibilities and escalation route.

No reviewer may be assigned while any mandatory competence, authorization or independence requirement remains unverified. If a suitable independent reviewer is unavailable, the report remains on hold; commercial or schedule pressure is not an exception.

The technical review is an internal quality-control step only. It is not a principal's qualification decision, certification, accreditation, statutory inspection, or regulatory approval. Report release authority must not be represented as authority to make those external decisions.


## 12. Data governance and information-security accountability (RA-08)

The following roles must be formally appointed with documented authority, competence, capacity and escalation routes before client evidence is accepted. Role labels in this plan do not constitute appointments.

| Control activity | Accountable role | Required separation / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Approve privacy/security policy, risk appetite, resources and exceptions | Management | Formal decision under MGT-07; residual-risk acceptance recorded |
| Assess purpose, necessity, party roles, notices, legal basis, rights and notification duties | Privacy / legal responsible person | Competent legal/privacy review; current requirements verified; advice and decision references retained |
| Approve systems, storage, channels, security baseline and incident response | Information-security responsible person | Technical capability and configuration evidence; supplier due diligence; independent verification where appropriate |
| Define engagement purpose, minimum data request and authorized recipients | Engagement owner | Approved scope, minimization rationale, client instructions and data inventory reference |
| Maintain data inventory, classification, transfer, access and disposal records | Data custodian / document controller | Restricted record access; traceable updates; no secrets stored in registers |
| Grant, review and revoke individual access | Designated access approver / system owner | Named account, least privilege, MFA where supported, time limit, review and revocation evidence |
| Coordinate suspected incidents and containment | Incident coordinator / security lead | Immediate escalation route, incident log, evidence preservation and tested contacts |
| Decide external notification and communications | Authorized management with privacy/legal advice | Documented legal/contract assessment, decision rationale and authorized spokesperson |
| Verify retention, return and secure deletion | Data custodian with legal/privacy and system owner | Approved schedule, legal-hold check, deletion/return evidence and supplier confirmation where applicable |

### Mandatory segregation and stop conditions

- The engagement owner must not unilaterally approve a new processing purpose, legal basis, supplier, cross-border transfer or high-risk exception.
- System administrators must not approve their own privileged access without an independent authorization and review.
- Users must not investigate or communicate externally about incidents beyond their delegated role.
- No real client evidence may be requested or received until MGT-07-related privacy/security controls, approved channels, access roles and retention/return arrangements are approved and verified.
- No unapproved personal email, personal cloud storage, public sharing link, public code repository or unapproved AI service may be used for client evidence.
- If data purpose, legal authority, security capability, recipient identity or safe storage cannot be demonstrated, pause collection and escalate; narrow scope or decline if unresolved.
- Any exception must be documented, risk-assessed, time-limited and approved by the authorized role; it cannot override legal requirements or hard-stop risks.
- A suspected incident triggers immediate internal reporting and the approved incident-response route. No one may conceal, delete or alter potential incident evidence.

Use the [Data Governance & Information Security Procedure](data-governance-information-security-procedure.md), [Data Asset Inventory & Classification Register](data-asset-inventory-register.md), [Access Authorization & Review Register](access-authorization-register.md), and [Data Incident Response Playbook](data-incident-response-playbook.md) as proposed controlled records. No role appointment, system approval, security control or incident capability is evidenced by this draft.


## 13. Client factual review governance (RA-09)

The client may identify factual inaccuracies in a controlled draft and submit authorized supporting information. The client does not approve TNCVCertification's findings or direct technical conclusions. The factual review coordinator preserves the exact draft version, original finding, client comment, evidence reference and disposition. The assessor records a reasoned decision; material changes affecting evidence sufficiency, findings or conclusions are independently re-reviewed by a competent technical reviewer. A separate delegated report approver authorizes release. No role is appointed by this draft, and no customer review is evidenced. See the [Client Factual Review & Correction Procedure](client-factual-review-procedure.md) and [Client Factual Correction Log](client-factual-correction-log.md).


## 14. Complaints handling governance (RA-10)

Complaint receipt, investigation, decision and corrective action must be separated so that the subject of a complaint cannot control its investigation or outcome. The complaint coordinator preserves the original complaint and communications; an independent investigator examines supporting and contradictory evidence; an independent decision reviewer approves the findings and response; corrective actions are assigned to owners and their effectiveness is checked by a separate verifier. Where internal independence is impracticable or senior management is implicated, appoint an external or otherwise independent route. Management must approve the complaint channel, acknowledgement/progress/final-response targets, decision authority, remedy limits and escalation route before customer use. No appointment, deadline or operational complaint channel is established by this draft. See the [Complaints Handling Procedure](complaints-handling-procedure.md) and [Complaints Register & Case Record](complaints-register-case-record.md).
