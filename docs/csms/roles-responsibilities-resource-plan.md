## 1. Purpose and boundaries

Define the functions, segregation controls and resource evidence needed to operate the proposed CSMS Readiness Review & Documentation Support service safely.

Role titles in this document are proposed functions, not evidence that a person has been appointed, is competent, or holds statutory, accreditation, principal or scheme-owner authorization. One person may cover more than one function only after a documented competence, capacity and impartiality review confirms that the combination is acceptable.

This framework does not authorize certification, principal qualification, official scoring or vendor approval.

## 2. Required functions

| Function | Core accountability | Minimum evidence before assignment |
|---|---|---|
| Management sponsor / service governance | Approve business model, resources, risk appetite, pilot and publication gates | Formal appointment and documented decision authority |
| Service owner | Own service scope, acceptance, capacity and operational performance | Approved role description, relevant service/process knowledge and delegated authority |
| Scheme and source owner | Identify principal requirement set, verify currentness, applicability and controlled source records | Demonstrated ability to interpret the exact source; independent verification arrangement |
| Assessor / reviewer | Conduct assigned document, interview or site review and record objective evidence | Assignment-specific competence file, authorization scope, conflict clearance and safety/site requirements |
| Technical reviewer | Independently review criteria, evidence reasoning, findings and report consistency | Technical competence for the scope; independence from the work reviewed |
| Impartiality / conflict reviewer | Evaluate threats, safeguards, role combinations and engagement restrictions | Independence, authority to restrict or reject engagements, documented evaluation |
| Engagement approver | Approve acceptance, scope, contract and resources before work starts | Delegated authority and evidence that all pre-engagement gates are closed |
| Report approver | Authorize release of the readiness report after technical and factual review | Approved authority, competence and independence appropriate to the report |
| Complaint handler | Receive, acknowledge, investigate and resolve complaints independently where practicable | Complaint-handling competence and no involvement in the matter under complaint |
| Document controller | Maintain controlled templates, source records, revisions, access and retention/disposal records | Document-control and information-security competence |
| Privacy / information-security responsible function | Review data purpose, access, transfer, storage, incidents and retention arrangements | Approved responsibility, relevant competence and escalation route |

## 3. Role segregation and incompatibility controls

1. The person who prepares or materially changes a client's evidence must not independently review that same work as an impartial readiness reviewer where this creates a conflict.
2. An assessor must not approve their own report as the sole technical reviewer.
3. A technical reviewer must not review work outside their demonstrated competence.
4. A complaint must be investigated by a person not responsible for the challenged activity wherever practicable. If this is not possible, an external or otherwise independent reviewer should be appointed.
5. The impartiality reviewer must have authority to require safeguards, restrict scope, reassign personnel or decline the engagement.
6. The engagement approver confirms that source, competence, impartiality, contract, privacy and resource gates are complete; the approver does not replace technical review.
7. Where a small team makes separation impracticable, document the threat, independent safeguard and rationale. If the threat cannot be reduced to an acceptable level, do not accept or continue the work.
8. Disclosure alone does not eliminate a conflict.

## 5. Assignment authorization checklist

Complete one record for every engagement before work begins:

- [ ] Engagement ID, client legal entity, principal/project, sector and work scope recorded.
- [ ] Applicable principal source pack identified, version checked and independently verified.
- [ ] Each assigned person's competence evidence reviewed against the exact tasks and scope.
- [ ] Mandatory statutory, client, principal or scheme-owner credentials independently verified where applicable.
- [ ] Any competence limitation, supervision requirement or scope restriction documented.
- [ ] Conflict declarations collected and reviewed; safeguards approved and effective.
- [ ] Assessor, technical reviewer, report approver and complaint route assigned with appropriate separation.
- [ ] Workload, availability, backup coverage and delivery time are realistic.
- [ ] Site access, induction, PPE and travel controls assessed if site work is included.
- [ ] Customer information handling, secure transfer, storage and access permissions approved.
- [ ] Engagement acceptance and resource approval recorded by the authorized role.
- [ ] No work starts until all mandatory items are closed or an explicitly authorized, justified exception is documented; no exception may override hard-stop rules.

Use the [CSMS Competence Matrix](competence-matrix.md) and [Impartiality & Conflict of Interest Register](impartiality-conflict-register.md) as supporting records.

## 8. Escalation and review

Escalate to management before acceptance or continuation where:
- A required role is vacant or lacks delegated authority.
- Competence or mandatory credentials cannot be demonstrated.
- Conflict safeguards are ineffective or independence cannot be maintained.
- Source authenticity, revision or applicability remains uncertain.
- Secure handling or lawful processing of customer data cannot be demonstrated.
- Workload, timing, budget or backup resources are inadequate.
- A complaint, serious error, data incident or material scope change affects service integrity.

Review this plan before the first pilot, after a material change, and at the management review interval once approved.
