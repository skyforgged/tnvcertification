## 1. Purpose and decision rule

This register prepares the management decisions required before the proposed CSMS Readiness Review & Documentation Support service can be piloted or launched.

Creating this register, a policy draft, or a completed template is not management approval. A decision is closed only when the authorized approver, decision date, scope, conditions, and objective approval record are entered and the evidence reference is retained.

**Default status for every item: Pending — no approval evidenced.** Do not infer approval from silence, discussion, document creation, or an assigned action owner.

## 3. Approval record format

For each decision, retain:

- Decision ID and exact question decided.
- Name and position of the authorized decision-maker.
- Decision: Approved / Approved with conditions / Rejected / Deferred.
- Scope, exclusions, conditions and review/expiry date where relevant.
- Date of decision and effective date, if different.
- Evidence reviewed and controlled record reference.
- Signature or traceable approval record.
- Follow-up actions, owners and due dates actually approved.

A conditional approval is not effective until its conditions are verified as satisfied by the designated independent reviewer.

## 4. Stop/go rules

The following are hard stops:

1. No customer engagement or pilot without an explicit MGT-10 approval and closure of its prerequisites.
2. No formal certification, principal qualification, official score, vendor approval, or recognition claim without separately verified authority and legal approval.
3. No assignment where competence or impartiality is unproven or safeguards are ineffective.
4. No transfer or processing of customer evidence until privacy, security, access and retention arrangements are approved.
5. No public activation while any mandatory approval gate remains open.
6. No retrospective or backdated approval record.

## 5. Current register state

## 6. Review and change control

The register owner should update this register after each formally recorded management decision. Changes to service scope, target principal, legal requirements, personnel, methodology, data processing or commercial terms must trigger review of affected decisions before implementation.
