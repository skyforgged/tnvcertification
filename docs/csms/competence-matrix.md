# CSMS Competence Matrix

**Status:** Internal working template — not an authorization or certification of personnel.  
**Reference:** TNCV-CSMS-COMP-001 (proposed)  
**Version:** 0.2 — RA-05 remediation draft  
**Owner:** [To be assigned]

## 1. Purpose and boundaries

Use this matrix to determine whether personnel have suitable, current and evidenced competence for the specific activities and scope assigned within the proposed CSMS Readiness Review & Documentation Support service.

This matrix does not itself qualify a person as an accredited auditor, certification decision-maker, principal-approved assessor, or legally authorized HSE professional. Any statutory, scheme-owner, client, or principal-specific qualification must be separately identified and verified with the issuing authority or scheme owner.

**Hard boundary:** no person may be assigned to an activity requiring a mandatory credential until the credential's validity, scope and identity have been independently checked. An internal rating cannot waive a mandatory requirement.

## 2. Competence dimensions and evidence

Assess each person against the actual assignment, not against a generic job title.

| Dimension | Evidence examples | Verification / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Education and professional background | Diploma/degree, professional registration, relevant technical qualification | Verify issuer, identity, validity and relevant subject |
| Sector and operational experience | Employment history, project references, role descriptions, verified work samples | Confirm relevance to sector, activity, risk and recency |
| HSE / CSMS technical knowledge | Training, examination, technical interview, demonstrated application | Map to actual work hazards and applicable criteria |
| Principal / scheme criteria knowledge | Controlled source review, clause-mapping exercise, knowledge assessment | Use the exact current source; record revision |
| Assessment methods | Observed document review, interview, site observation, sampling exercise | Record observed task, evaluator and outcome |
| Evidence evaluation and reporting | Anonymized work sample, report review, finding-writing exercise | Assess accuracy, traceability, neutrality and limitations |
| Legal / regulatory awareness | Relevant training, professional experience, demonstrated legal-register interpretation | Define jurisdiction and scope; escalate legal interpretation |
| Ethics, impartiality and confidentiality | Declaration, conflict screening, training and scenario assessment | Engagement-specific conflict screening remains mandatory |
| Site and task-specific readiness | Site induction, required PPE / safety training, task-specific authorization | Confirm before site activity |
| Mandatory external credentials | Licence, registration, client/principal credential or scheme authorization | Verify directly with issuer / authorized source; record expiry and scope |

Attendance at a course, a CV statement, self-declaration or possession of an unverified certificate is not by itself sufficient evidence of competence.

## 3. Competence rating

| Rating | Definition | Permitted use |
|---|---|---|
| 0 — Not evidenced | No reliable evidence available | Not assignable |
| 1 — Awareness | Basic awareness; requires close supervision | Limited support tasks only, if approved |
| 2 — Working | Can perform defined tasks with supervision and review | Restricted tasks under named supervision |
| 3 — Independent | Can perform the defined task independently within approved scope | Independent assignment within documented authorization |
| 4 — Technical lead | Can lead the activity and review others' work within demonstrated competence | Lead/reviewer role only for proven technical scope |

Ratings are internal resource-planning indicators only. They are not externally recognized grades and must not replace mandatory qualifications. A rating must be supported by evidence and evaluator/date; do not average unrelated dimensions into a single pass score.

Acceptable effectiveness evidence may include:
- Observed completion of a representative task against defined criteria.
- Independent review of a sample requirement matrix, assessment record or report.
- Supervised engagement with documented feedback and closure of identified gaps.
- Demonstrated interpretation of a principal's current controlled requirement source.
- Verification of a mandatory credential with its issuing authority.

Course attendance alone does not establish competence. Where supervision is required, identify the supervisor and the tasks the person may not perform independently.

## 7. Technical reviewer independence and role separation

1. A person must not be the sole technical reviewer of their own assessment work or report.
2. The reviewer must have competence relevant to the criteria, sector and activity being reviewed.
3. The reviewer must not have an unresolved conflict that could impair objective review.
4. If the team cannot provide an appropriately independent and competent reviewer, hold the report or obtain an approved external reviewer; do not waive the control for convenience.
5. Record reviewer comments, corrections, unresolved disagreements, disposition and final approval reference.
6. Report approval is an internal release authority and does not constitute principal qualification, certification or regulatory approval.

## 8. Monitoring, expiry and reassessment

Review competence and authorization:
- Before the person's first assignment.
- Before any new principal, sector, work activity, site type or materially changed criteria.
- Before expiry of a mandatory credential and whenever issuer status changes.
- Following significant errors, complaints, adverse feedback or performance concerns.
- At the approved periodic review interval.

Restrict or suspend assignment where evidence is missing, outdated, unverifiable, outside scope or contradicted by performance. Record the reason, effective date, affected assignments and reinstatement evidence.

## 9. Records, privacy and access

Maintain only information necessary to demonstrate competence and authorization. Restrict access to designated management and authorized reviewers. Store credential copies, experience records, verification notes and evaluation results under approved document-control, privacy and retention rules. Do not place unnecessary personal data in engagement reports.

### Pre-assignment checklist

- [ ] Assignment scope, tasks, principal, sector, site and criteria are defined.
- [ ] Competence evidence is mapped to each assigned task.
- [ ] Mandatory credentials and their validity/scope are independently verified.
- [ ] Experience and task performance evidence are reviewed, not merely self-declared.
- [ ] Gaps, supervision needs and restrictions are documented.
- [ ] Engagement-specific impartiality/conflict screening is complete.
- [ ] Technical reviewer competence and independence are confirmed.
- [ ] Workload, availability and required site induction/safety arrangements are confirmed.
- [ ] Authorization is signed/approved by a person with documented delegated authority.
- [ ] Records are stored in the approved restricted-access location.

**Pre-launch gate:** competence criteria, mandatory credentials, actual personnel records, assessor pool, reviewer arrangements and delegated approval authority must be formally approved and evidenced before customer delivery. This template alone does not close RA-05.
