## 1. Purpose and boundaries

Use this matrix to determine whether personnel have suitable competence for the specific activities and scope assigned within the proposed CSMS Readiness Review & Documentation Support service.

This matrix does not itself qualify a person as an accredited auditor, certification decision-maker, principal-approved assessor, or legally authorized HSE professional. Any statutory, scheme-owner, client, or principal-specific qualification must be separately identified and verified.

## 2. Competence dimensions

Assess competence against the actual assignment. Relevant dimensions may include:

- Education, professional background, and relevant technical knowledge.
- Work experience in the relevant sector, work activities, and contractor risk profile.
- Understanding of the named principal's current CSMS requirements and source documents.
- Ability to review HSE management-system documentation and evidence objectively.
- Interviewing, observation, sampling, evidence traceability, and report-writing skills.
- Applicable legal/regulatory awareness, within the defined scope.
- Confidentiality, information security, ethics, and impartiality awareness.
- Any mandatory licence, registration, training, or authorization required for the assignment.

## 3. Competence rating

| Rating | Definition |
|---|---|
| 0 — Not evidenced | No evidence available to support competence |
| 1 — Awareness | Basic awareness; requires close supervision |
| 2 — Working | Can perform defined tasks with supervision/review |
| 3 — Independent | Can perform the defined task independently within approved scope |
| 4 — Technical lead | Can lead the activity and review others' work within demonstrated competence |

Ratings are internal resource-planning indicators only. They are not externally recognized grades and must not replace mandatory qualifications.

Possible effectiveness evidence:
- Observed completion of a representative task.
- Review of a sample matrix or report.
- Supervised engagement with documented feedback.
- Demonstrated understanding of a principal's current requirement document.
- Verification of any required formal credential from its issuing authority.

Attendance at a course alone does not establish competence.

## 7. Periodic review and reassignment

Review competence:
- Before first assignment.
- When scope, principal criteria, sector, or work activity changes.
- Following significant report errors, complaints, or performance concerns.
- When credentials expire or mandatory requirements change.
- At the organization's approved periodic review interval.

Restrict or suspend assignment where competence evidence is missing, outdated, or outside the person's demonstrated scope.

## 8. Records and privacy

Maintain only information necessary to demonstrate competence and authorization. Restrict access to designated management personnel. Store credential copies, experience records, training evidence, and evaluation notes under the approved document-control and retention rules. Apply applicable privacy requirements and retention periods.
