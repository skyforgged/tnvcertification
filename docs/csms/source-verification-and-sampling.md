## F. Evidence intake and sufficiency distinction

For each evidence item, separately record:
1. **Received:** the item was supplied or made accessible.
2. **Examined:** the assessor actually reviewed the identified version/content.
3. **Sufficient for the criterion:** the examined evidence adequately supports the specific requirement within the review scope.
