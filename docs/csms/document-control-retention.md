# CSMS Document Control & Record Retention

**Status:** Internal working framework — align with the organization's approved information-security, privacy, and records policies before use.  
**Reference:** TNCV-CSMS-DOC-001 (proposed)  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Owner:** [Document Controller]

## 1. Purpose and scope

Define controls for creating, approving, identifying, accessing, changing, storing, retaining, and disposing of documents and records used in the proposed CSMS Readiness Review & Documentation Support service.

This framework applies to:
- Internal procedures, templates, checklists, and guidance.
- Principal/client requirement sources and revisions.
- Client-provided evidence and working notes.
- Competence, impartiality, contract-review, and assessment records.
- Findings, technical reviews, approvals, issued reports, and correspondence.

It is a proposed service-specific control and does not replace applicable law, contractual obligations, or the company's enterprise document-control policy.

## 2. Document categories

| Category | Examples | Control expectation |
|---|---|---|
| External criteria | Principal CSMS manuals, tender instructions, portal forms, legal references | Record source, owner, revision/date, access date, applicability, and controlled-copy status |
| Controlled internal documents | Procedures, approved forms, policies, work instructions | Unique ID, version, owner, approver, effective date, revision history |
| Client evidence | HSE policies, risk assessments, training records, incident records | Preserve received version and provenance; access restricted |
| Working records | Review notes, sampling logs, draft findings, correspondence | Traceable to engagement; protect from unauthorized alteration |
| Issued deliverables | Final reports, approved matrices, formal correspondence | Approved version, issue date, recipients, distribution record |
| Superseded / archived records | Replaced templates and prior reports | Retain as required; clearly mark obsolete or superseded |

## 3. Document identification and approval

Each controlled internal document should contain:
- Document title and unique identifier.
- Version or revision number.
- Document owner.
- Prepared, reviewed, and approved roles.
- Approval and effective dates.
- Revision history and change summary.
- Classification or handling label where required.

A document is not effective merely because it exists in the repository. It becomes effective only after the designated approval and release process is complete. Until then, mark it “Draft”, “Internal working document”, or “Not approved for operational use”.

## 4. External requirement source control

For every principal/client requirement source, record:
- Exact document title and issuing organization.
- Document number, revision, issue/effective date.
- Original source or controlled-copy reference.
- Date retrieved and person who verified it.
- Project, contractor type, work scope, and other applicability conditions.
- Known amendments, portal updates, FAQs, appendices, and clarifications.
- Whether the copy is controlled, client-provided, or publicly available.

Recheck currentness before each engagement and whenever the client/principal notifies a change. Do not silently replace the criteria mid-assessment. Changes must be evaluated, recorded, and agreed with the client where they affect scope or findings.

## 5. Access, security, and confidentiality

Apply least-privilege access:
- Grant access only to personnel assigned to the engagement or control function.
- Use approved storage and transfer channels.
- Avoid sending sensitive evidence through unapproved personal accounts or public links.
- Apply access controls, authentication, and backup requirements under company policy.
- Do not reuse client evidence for marketing, training, demonstrations, or another client engagement without documented authorization and suitable anonymization.
- Record disclosure, transfer, or access incidents and escalate under the organization's incident process.

Classify client information according to the company's approved classification scheme. If none exists, obtain management approval for a temporary classification before receiving sensitive evidence.

## 6. Record integrity and traceability

For records relied upon in a review:
- Preserve the received file or a controlled reference to it.
- Record source, receipt date, version/date, and evidence ID.
- Keep reviewer annotations distinguishable from original client content.
- Record any transformation, redaction, extraction, or correction.
- Restrict deletion or replacement of engagement records.
- Maintain links between requirement clauses, evidence IDs, findings, and report references.

Never fabricate, backdate, overwrite, or present a newly created record as evidence of historical implementation or approval.

## 8. Legal hold, client return, and secure disposal

Where a complaint, dispute, investigation, audit, legal hold, or regulatory requirement applies, suspend routine disposal for affected records until the hold is formally released.

At contract closeout:
- Return or securely dispose of client materials as agreed and legally permitted.
- Retain only records required by approved policy, law, or contract.
- Record what was returned, retained, or destroyed, by whom, and when.
- Ensure backups and replicas are addressed under the approved deletion process.

## 9. Revision and obsolete-document control

The document controller maintains a master register containing document ID, title, owner, version, approval status, effective date, review date, and storage location.

Changes require review and approval. Superseded versions must be removed from active use or clearly marked obsolete while preserving required history. Staff must be able to identify the current approved version before use.

## 11. Approval and implementation gates

Before operational use, management must approve:
1. Document classification and authorized storage channels.
2. Access roles and information-security controls.
3. Retention periods and legal/contractual basis for each record class.
4. Client return, deletion, backup, and legal-hold process.
5. Document approval authority and review frequency.
6. Privacy and confidentiality notices and contract clauses.

**Until these items are approved, this framework is an internal draft and the proposed retention periods remain intentionally blank.**

## 12. RA-08 data governance and information-security linkage

The document-control and retention framework is the record-control foundation; the more detailed privacy, data-flow, access and incident controls are defined in the [Data Governance & Information Security Procedure](data-governance-information-security-procedure.md). Use its linked [Data Asset Inventory & Classification Register](data-asset-inventory-register.md), [Access Authorization & Review Register](access-authorization-register.md) and [Data Incident Response Playbook](data-incident-response-playbook.md) for engagement-specific evidence.

The proposed classification and control requirements in those documents are not effective until management approves the enterprise classification scheme or an aligned service-specific scheme, privacy/legal review, approved systems and channels, access model, supplier arrangements, incident contacts and retention schedule. The placeholder retention periods in Section 7 remain unapproved; this update does not create or imply a retention period.


## 13. RA-09 client factual review records

The [Client Factual Review & Correction Procedure](client-factual-review-procedure.md) governs controlled draft review, comment evaluation, reasoned disposition, technical escalation, revision history and release. Use the [Client Factual Correction Log](client-factual-correction-log.md) as the engagement record. Preserve the exact draft sent, original findings, client comments, supporting evidence references, disposition rationale, revised versions, technical re-review and delegated release evidence. These records remain subject to approved classification, access, secure transfer and retention controls; this draft does not approve any channel or retention period.
