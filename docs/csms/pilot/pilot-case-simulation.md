## 6. Pilot limitations

- Desk-top simulation only; no real client, principal, site, workforce or operational evidence was examined.
- All criteria and records are invented; no source verification against a real principal was performed.
- No interviews, site observations, record authenticity checks, legal review or effectiveness testing occurred.

- No assessor competence or independence is demonstrated by completing this simulation.
