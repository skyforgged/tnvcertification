## 2. Evidence status vocabulary

Use evidence status rather than an invented universal CSMS score.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not reviewed | Evidence has not yet been examined |
| Evidence available | A relevant record was supplied; adequacy still requires review |
| Partially evidenced | Some elements of the requirement are supported, but gaps remain |
| Evidence missing | No relevant evidence was supplied or located |
| Not applicable | Applicability was assessed and the reason is documented |
| Requires clarification | Requirement or evidence is ambiguous and needs confirmation |

Do not use “pass”, “fail”, “approved”, “qualified”, or a principal's official score unless the scheme owner has explicitly authorized that terminology and decision.

## 6. Quality and traceability checks

- [ ] Requirement document is from an identifiable source and its revision/date is recorded.
- [ ] Each mapped row points to a clause, page, or other traceable source reference.
- [ ] Evidence references identify document title, version/date, and owner where possible.
- [ ] Applicability decisions include a reason.
- [ ] Missing information is distinguished from nonconformity.
- [ ] Review limitations and sampling boundaries are recorded.
- [ ] Reviewer and technical reviewer are identified.
- [ ] No principal logo, approval statement, or official scoring terminology is used without written permission.

## 7. Disclaimer for internal use

This matrix supports a scoped document/readiness review only. It does not itself establish compliance, certification, contractor qualification, vendor approval, or acceptance by a principal. The principal/client retains its own decision-making authority unless a separate, documented authorization states otherwise.
