# CSMS Client Factual Review & Correction Procedure

**Status:** Internal working draft — not approved for customer delivery.  
**Proposed reference:** TNCV-CSMS-PRO-FR-001  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Owner:** [To be assigned]  
**Approval authority:** [Management to assign]

## 1. Purpose

Define a fair, traceable and controlled opportunity for a client to review factual accuracy in a **draft** CSMS Readiness Review report before release. The process is limited to facts, source references, evidence identity/context, dates, names, scope descriptions and transcription or calculation errors.

Client factual review is not a negotiation of findings, a conformity decision, technical review, appeal, certification decision or principal qualification process. The client may identify an error and submit supporting information; TNCVCertification retains responsibility for independently evaluating the information and determining the report's evidence-based wording.

## 2. Mandatory boundaries

1. Send only a clearly marked draft to authorized client recipients after applicable privacy/security and engagement gates have been approved.
2. State the review purpose, draft version, response route and agreed response deadline in the transmittal. Do not invent a default deadline; use the contract or a mutually recorded period.
3. Ask the client to identify the exact report section/finding, disputed statement, proposed correction and supporting source/evidence.
4. Do not request unnecessary personal, sensitive, security-restricted or third-party information. Apply the approved data-governance procedure and secure channel.
5. Do not silently overwrite the original finding, source evidence, prior draft or client comment.
6. Do not accept a requested change solely because the client requests it, nor reject it solely because it challenges the report.
7. A factual correction does not automatically change the assessment conclusion. Any effect on criteria, evidence sufficiency, finding status or conclusion requires documented assessor evaluation and, where material, technical re-review.
8. Client non-response does not constitute factual confirmation or acceptance. Record the non-response and proceed only under the agreed engagement terms and approval gates.
9. Do not release the report while a material factual issue, required review, privacy restriction or approval remains unresolved.

## 3. Roles and separation

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Client factual reviewer | Identify factual inaccuracies and provide relevant, authorized supporting evidence; does not approve TNCVCertification findings |
| Engagement coordinator | Issue controlled draft, verify recipient authority, set/record response period and log receipt |
| Assessor / report author | Evaluate each comment against original evidence and criteria; propose a reasoned disposition |
| Technical reviewer | Re-review changes that may affect technical interpretation, evidence sufficiency, findings or conclusions |
| Impartiality reviewer | Resolve conflict concerns or pressure to alter findings; require safeguards or hold the engagement |
| Report approver | Confirm all factual comments are dispositioned and authorize release under separate delegation |
| Document controller | Preserve versions, source records, comment log, decisions and distribution evidence |

The author must not independently approve their own material change. Where team size prevents full separation, record the threat and an independent safeguard; if no effective safeguard exists, hold release.

## 4. Draft transmittal and client instructions

The transmittal record must identify:
- Engagement ID, report ID and exact draft version/date.
- Authorized client recipient(s) and secure delivery reference.
- Purpose: factual accuracy review only.
- Response deadline agreed for the engagement and response channel.
- Requested comment format or reference to the [Client Factual Correction Log](client-factual-correction-log.md).
- Statement that the client may provide corrections and supporting evidence but cannot direct removal of a substantiated finding or dictate a technical conclusion.
- Confidentiality, permitted use and handling instructions for the draft.

A draft must be watermarked or prominently labeled **DRAFT — NOT FOR RELIANCE / NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION** as appropriate to the approved document-control policy.

## 5. Comment intake and preservation

For each comment:
1. Assign a unique comment ID and record receipt date, sender and authority, affected report version, page/section/finding ID and exact text challenged.
2. Preserve the original report statement and the exact client comment without paraphrasing it as the source record.
3. Record the correction requested and supporting evidence/source reference, version/date, provenance and secure storage location.
4. Check whether the material is within scope, authorized for use, relevant, authentic enough to examine and permitted under privacy/confidentiality controls.
5. If evidence is missing, unclear or unsafe to receive, request clarification through an approved channel or record why it cannot be considered.
6. Link each comment to affected requirement, evidence ID, finding ID and report location where applicable.

Do not place sensitive evidence or credentials in the correction log itself; use controlled references and restricted storage.

## 6. Evaluation and disposition

The assessor records one of these outcomes for each comment:

- **Accepted:** the original report contains a factual error; correct it and cite the supporting basis.
- **Partly accepted:** correct only the substantiated part; explain what remains unchanged.
- **Not accepted:** retain the original statement and explain why the comment/evidence does not establish a factual error.
- **Clarification required:** request specific additional information; identify owner and due date.
- **Out of scope / not factual:** route a technical disagreement, request to change criteria, commercial dispute or complaint to the appropriate controlled process.

The rationale must be specific and evidence-based. Record whether the change affects the evidence record, finding, risk/priority, recommendation, conclusion, limitations or only editorial wording. A client preference, commercial pressure, unsupported assertion or desire to improve a tender outcome is not an evidential basis for changing a finding.

If new information changes the factual basis or technical interpretation, update the controlled assessment records and perform the necessary reassessment. Route material technical changes through the independent technical-review procedure and preserve the before/after versions.

## 7. Technical disagreements, complaints and escalation

A disagreement about interpretation of a criterion, sufficiency of evidence, sampling, finding classification or conclusion is not treated as a simple factual correction. Record the issue and refer it to the technical reviewer/technical authority under the approved review procedure.

Allegations of misconduct, unfair treatment, service failure, confidentiality breach or dissatisfaction with the process must be registered under the complaint process. A client dispute with a principal's score, procurement decision or qualification outcome must be directed to that principal/client; TNCVCertification has no authority to decide it.

If a comment reveals a conflict, coercion, falsification request, unauthorized disclosure or data incident, stop the affected activity and escalate under the relevant impartiality, evidence-integrity or incident process.

## 8. Revision, verification and report release

1. Maintain a revision history linking each accepted/partly accepted comment to the exact report version and changed text.
2. Preserve the original draft and all prior versions as controlled records; do not overwrite them.
3. The author records corrections and references the evidence basis.
4. The technical reviewer verifies material changes and records re-review outcome.
5. The report approver confirms every comment has a disposition, blocking issues are closed, the final version is consistent with the reviewed version, and distribution is authorized.
6. Issue only the approved final version through controlled distribution. Retain recipient list and issue date.
7. If a correction is needed after issue, use a controlled revised report or formal addendum, retain the superseded version and reason, and notify affected recipients under the approved process.

## 9. Required records

Retain, subject to approved retention and access controls:
- Draft transmittal and authorized recipient record.
- Exact draft version sent and secure delivery reference.
- Original client comments and supporting evidence references.
- Completed [Client Factual Correction Log](client-factual-correction-log.md).
- Assessor dispositions and rationale.
- Technical re-review/escalation records where applicable.
- Final report revision history, approval and distribution.
- Non-response or unresolved issue record, where applicable.

## 10. Closure and effectiveness

A factual review cycle is complete only when:
- Every received comment has a unique ID and recorded disposition.
- Supporting evidence and provenance are referenced and appropriately assessed.
- Accepted changes are reflected in a controlled version; rejected/partial comments have reasoned explanations.
- Material technical changes have been independently re-reviewed.
- No unresolved release-blocking issue remains, or the report is held.
- A separately delegated report approver authorizes release and the document controller retains the record.

For RA-09 remediation closure, the organization must additionally demonstrate management-approved procedure and role authority, at least one authorized real engagement factual-review record, preserved original and revised versions, complete comment/evidence/disposition traceability, independent review of material changes, controlled release, and independent closure verification. A blank template, simulated example, or client signature alone is insufficient.

## 11. Approval status

This draft creates no authority or operational readiness. Management approval, role appointments, secure channels, data-governance gate, contract terms and actual engagement records remain to be evidenced before customer use.
