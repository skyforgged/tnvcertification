## E1. Evidence intake, examination and sufficiency register (RA-03)

Use one row per evidence item. Do not collapse receipt, examination and sufficiency into a single status.

| Evidence ID | Requirement ID(s) | Title / owner / source | Version / date | Received by / date | Examined by / date | Integrity / authenticity checks | Sufficiency decision | Rationale / limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EV-001 | [REQ-###] | [Complete] | [Complete] | [Complete] | [Complete] | [Complete / not possible + reason] | [Sufficient / partial / insufficient / not assessed] | [Criterion-specific reason] |

**Decision rules**
- **Received** means the item was supplied or made accessible; it does not mean it was read or verified.
- **Examined** means the identified version/content was actually reviewed and the reviewer/date are recorded.
- **Sufficient** means the examined evidence adequately supports the specific requirement within the agreed scope. State the criterion-specific rationale and limits.
- An unsigned, expired, incomplete, contradictory or unapproved record may be examined but still be insufficient. Explain why.
- If authenticity or integrity cannot be checked, state that limitation; do not imply verification.
- Do not infer implementation or effectiveness from a policy, procedure or blank form alone.
