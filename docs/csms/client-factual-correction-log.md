## C. Disposition rules

- **Accepted:** identify the factual error, evidence basis and exact correction.
- **Partly accepted:** identify the accepted portion and why the remainder is unchanged.
- **Not accepted:** explain why the evidence/comment does not establish a factual error.
- **Clarification required:** state the precise information needed, owner and due date.
- **Out of scope / not factual:** record the route to technical disagreement, complaint or principal/client dispute process.

A request to change a technical conclusion, criterion interpretation, evidence sufficiency or finding classification must not be treated as a simple factual correction. Refer it for independent technical review. Do not remove or rewrite the original finding without preserving its prior wording and a reasoned decision trail.

## F. Non-response or unresolved comments

If the client does not respond by the agreed date, record the transmittal, delivery evidence, reminders (if any), no-response status and contractual basis for next steps. Non-response is not factual confirmation. Hold the report if the engagement terms, material uncertainty or approval requirements do not permit release.

For unresolved comments, record the issue, reason, responsible authority and decision to hold, narrow, escalate or proceed only where the approved procedure and contract permit. Never imply client agreement where none exists.
