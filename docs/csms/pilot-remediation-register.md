## Closure requirements

An action may be closed only when:
1. The accountable owner is assigned and has completed the action.
2. Objective implementation evidence is linked (approved procedure, completed records, training/briefing record, system configuration or test result as applicable).
3. An independent reviewer checks the evidence and records a decision.
4. Any residual risk or limitation is accepted by the authorized management role.
5. The status, closure date and evidence references are recorded.

## Pilot re-entry gate

A real customer pilot remains **not authorized** until management documents approval of the service model, verified principal criteria, legal/claims review, competent personnel, impartiality safeguards, methodology, data governance, commercial terms and report authorization. The ten actions above are necessary remediation items but do not replace those broader pre-launch approvals.

## RA-05 — Competence and personnel authorization evidence criteria

**Current status: OPEN.** The competence matrix is a strengthened working template only. No individual has been appointed or authorized by this register, and no credential or experience claim is verified by the existence of the template.

### Required implementation evidence

1. Management-approved role descriptions, competence criteria and delegated authorization authority.
2. Named personnel register with relevant education, qualifications, sector/activity experience and task-specific evidence.
3. Independent verification record for every mandatory licence, registration, client/principal credential or scheme authorization, including issuer, validity, scope and expiry where applicable.
4. Demonstrated competence evidence (for example, observed task, reviewed work sample, supervised assignment and documented evaluator decision); course attendance alone is insufficient.
5. Engagement-specific assignment authorization mapping each person to permitted tasks, scope, supervision and restrictions.
6. Evidence that the technical reviewer is competent for the relevant scope and independent from the work reviewed.
7. Conflict screening, site induction/safety readiness where relevant, and controlled storage of personnel records.
8. Independent closure review confirming the above evidence is complete and current.

### Closure decision

RA-05 may be marked **Closed** only after actual personnel and assignment records exist, mandatory credentials have been verified with reliable issuing sources, authorization is approved by a delegated authority, and an independent reviewer records acceptance. Draft criteria, blank forms, hypothetical names, training plans or statements in chat do not satisfy closure.

## RA-06 — Impartiality and conflict-of-interest evidence criteria

**Current status: OPEN.** The framework and role controls have been strengthened, but no actual engagement declarations, threat evaluations, safeguard implementation or independent approvals are evidenced by this document update.

1. Management-approved impartiality policy, threat evaluation criteria, hard-stop conditions, fee restrictions and escalation authority.
2. Formal appointment and delegation for an impartiality / compliance reviewer with authority independent of sales and delivery outcomes.

## RA-07 — Technical review and quality assurance evidence criteria

**Current status: OPEN.** Draft procedure, checklist, register and role-governance wording have been added as documentation controls. They do not demonstrate management approval, an appointed reviewer, a completed engagement review or an authorized report release.

### Required implementation evidence

1. Management-approved technical review procedure, reviewer competence criteria, role segregation, escalation authority, record controls and report-release delegation.
2. Named reviewer appointment(s) with defined scope, verified relevant qualifications/experience, demonstrated review competence, current task authorization, capacity and restrictions.
3. Engagement-specific conflict declaration and impartiality clearance proving the reviewer is independent of the assessment, report authorship and any conflicting advisory/implementation work.
4. At least one completed, authorized engagement technical review using the approved checklist, with the exact reviewed report ID/version, complete review inputs, criterion-by-criterion results, rationale for N/A items and reviewer sign-off.
5. Review comment/correction log with unique IDs, severity, affected sections/evidence, author response, supporting evidence, reviewer verification and closure of all release-blocking issues.
6. Technical disagreement/escalation record for any disagreement that actually arose, including independent authority competence, conflict check, evidence, decision and rationale. If none arose, document the review-specific basis for no escalation.
7. Separately authorized report approver and release record tied to the exact reviewed report version, release decision, date and controlled distribution record.
8. Controlled revision history showing changes after review and evidence of re-review where a change could affect findings, conclusions or intended use.
9. Independent closure verification confirming evidence authenticity, completeness, currentness, traceability, authorization and effectiveness.

### Closure decision

RA-07 may be marked **Closed** only after the required procedure and delegations are formally approved, reviewer competence and independence are verified for the relevant scope, at least one real authorized engagement has a complete accepted review record, all release-blocking comments are closed, and report release is authorized by a separately delegated approver. An independent closure reviewer must record acceptance.

A blank checklist, draft procedure, hypothetical simulation, reviewer name without competence/appointment evidence, unsigned report, or technical review signature without a separate release authorization does not satisfy closure. Technical review does not confer certification, principal qualification or regulatory authority.

## RA-08 — Data governance and information-security evidence criteria

**Current status: OPEN.** Draft procedure, inventory/classification template, access register, incident playbook and role controls have been added. They do not prove lawful processing, approved systems, configured safeguards, trained personnel, tested incident response or management approval.

### Required implementation evidence

1. Management-approved privacy and information-security policy/standard, data classification and handling scheme, risk appetite, control owners, exception authority and MGT-07 decision record.
2. Legal/privacy review for the proposed service and each relevant engagement: defined purpose and necessity, applicable legal basis/authority, notices, data-subject handling, contractual terms, party roles and responsibilities, disclosure and any cross-border access/transfer. Retain the legal review reference and approved client instructions.
3. Completed data inventory and flow map covering categories, data subjects, source, purpose, classification, volume, systems/locations, users, recipients, suppliers/subprocessors, transfer locations, retention trigger and disposal route.
4. Data minimization decisions and approved evidence request list, including redaction/masking or sampling alternatives for personal, sensitive, security or excessive information.
5. Approved storage and transfer systems/channels with objective configuration/capability evidence appropriate to classification (for example access controls, MFA where supported, encryption, audit logs, backup/recovery, endpoint controls and deletion capability). Supplier due diligence, hosting/subprocessor locations and contract terms where relevant.
6. Named access appointments and least-privilege records; verified account settings, access reviews, privileged access controls, revocation evidence and external sharing logs.
7. Approved retention schedule with legal/contractual basis, legal-hold process, return/deletion procedure, backup treatment and actual disposal evidence; no placeholder period may be treated as approved.
8. Approved incident response roles, current contact/escalation list, legal notification assessment route, communication authority and evidence-preservation method.
9. Completed tabletop exercise(s) for material scenarios such as misdirected client evidence, compromised account and supplier incident; record decisions, timing, gaps, corrective actions and effectiveness re-test.
10. Approved supplier/processor agreements and periodic review where third parties can access or store information.
11. User confidentiality commitments and role-appropriate privacy/security awareness with attendance and effectiveness evidence.
12. Independent verification of the complete control package, including configuration, test records, risk treatment and residual-risk acceptance by authorized management.

A draft policy, blank register, vendor marketing statement, unchecked configuration list, generic confidentiality clause, planned training, or untested incident playbook is not sufficient. Do not receive real client evidence before the mandatory privacy/security gate is approved and verified.

## RA-09 — Client factual review evidence criteria

**Current status: OPEN.** The draft procedure and blank correction log have been prepared and linked to the report template and operational procedure. No management approval, authorized client review, completed correction record, or engagement evidence is established by these documents.

### Required implementation evidence

1. Management-approved client factual review procedure, defined role authority, review boundary, escalation route and release controls.
2. Contract/engagement terms defining the draft factual-review opportunity, authorized client recipient(s), secure communication route and an agreed response period. Do not apply an invented universal deadline.
3. Evidence that the exact draft report version was transmitted to an authorized client representative and was clearly labeled as a draft, with the factual-only purpose and response instructions.
4. Completed engagement-specific correction log with unique comment IDs, receipt dates, exact affected report version/section/finding, preserved original wording, exact client comment, requested correction and supporting evidence references/provenance.
5. A reasoned disposition for every comment (accepted, partly accepted, not accepted, clarification required or out of scope/not factual), including evidence basis and effect on evidence, finding, recommendation, conclusion or editorial wording.
6. Controlled report revision history linking accepted corrections to the exact revised version; preserve the original draft and prior versions without silent overwrite.
7. Independent technical re-review of changes affecting criteria interpretation, evidence sufficiency, findings or conclusions; record why re-review is not applicable for purely editorial/factual changes where appropriate.
8. Documented handling of non-response, unresolved comments, technical disagreements, complaints and principal/client disputes. Non-response must not be represented as factual confirmation.
9. Separate delegated report approval tied to the exact final version and controlled distribution record; no release while blocking issues remain.
10. Independent closure verification confirming completeness, authenticity, traceability, authorization, privacy/security handling and effectiveness of the factual-review process.

### Closure decision

RA-09 may be marked **Closed** only after the procedure and authority are approved, at least one authorized real engagement demonstrates a complete factual-review cycle, all comments have reasoned dispositions, original and revised versions are preserved, material technical changes are independently re-reviewed, the exact final version is separately authorized and distributed, and an independent closure reviewer records acceptance.

A blank log, simulated case, client signature alone, undocumented verbal agreement, or statement that the client had “no comments” without a controlled transmittal/non-response record does not close RA-09. Client factual review is not client approval of findings and does not confer certification, principal qualification or appeal authority.

## RA-10 — Complaints handling evidence criteria

**Current status: OPEN.** A draft complaints procedure and blank restricted case-record template have been added and linked to the operational procedure, role framework, document master register and MGT-09 approval requirements. These documents do not establish an approved complaint route, appointed independent investigator, approved response targets or a complaint investigated in practice.

### Required implementation evidence

1. Management-approved complaints policy/procedure defining scope, accessible intake routes, confidentiality, no-retaliation safeguards, triage, investigation, decision, response, escalation, reopening and records.
2. Approved and communicated complaint channels, including a safe method for verbal/written complaints and a documented approach to anonymous complaints.
3. Management-approved acknowledgement, triage, progress-update and final-response targets, with authority and process for justified extensions. Targets must reflect applicable legal/contractual duties and actual resources; do not invent or backdate them.
4. Formal appointments and delegated authority for complaint coordinator, investigator, independent decision reviewer, management escalation authority, corrective-action owner and effectiveness verifier.
5. Competence and conflict/independence evidence for actual case roles. The subject of the complaint must not control investigation, findings, response or record access; external/independent route must be available where internal separation is impracticable or management is implicated.
6. At least one actual complaint case record (if a complaint has been received) showing original complaint wording, unique ID, receipt, acknowledgement, triage, risk and conflict screening, safe communication and evidence preservation. If no complaint has been received, do not fabricate a case; demonstrate the approved route through a controlled exercise and retain real-case readiness evidence.
7. Completed investigation plan and evidence index for an actual case or approved exercise, including supporting and contradictory evidence, interviews, limitations, fair opportunity to respond and specialist referrals where needed.
8. Independent review and documented decision for each allegation, with substantiated/partly substantiated/not substantiated/unable to determine/out-of-scope rationale and an approved response.
9. Correction and corrective-action records with root cause, owner, approved due date, implementation evidence, residual risk and independent effectiveness verification. Keep open actions tracked even if the complaint is administratively closed.
10. Evidence of appropriate handling of technical disagreement, RA-09 factual correction, principal/client decision dispute, privacy/security incident, legal concern and external referral without conflating authorities or delaying urgent response.
11. Restricted complaint-record access, approved secure intake/storage, retention/legal-hold/disposal arrangements and protection of complainant/third-party information.
12. Trend monitoring and management review using appropriate aggregated/anonymized data, with decisions, owners and follow-up effectiveness evidence.
13. Independent closure verification confirming procedure approval, role independence, target governance, case/route evidence, response quality, corrective-action effectiveness and record integrity.

### Closure decision

RA-10 may be marked **Closed** only when management has approved and communicated the complaint process and targets; competent independent roles and an escalation fallback are appointed; approved secure intake and record controls are operational; and objective evidence demonstrates the process works. At least one real complaint should be evaluated where one has been received. If no complaint has arisen, a controlled exercise can test readiness but must not be represented as a real complaint or as evidence of actual customer case handling. The exercise does not replace approvals, appointments, live channel verification or ongoing monitoring. An independent reviewer must verify the full package and management must accept residual risks.

A blank template, draft procedure, unapproved email address, invented response deadline, self-investigation by the subject, unsigned response, or completed action without independent effectiveness verification does not close RA-10.
