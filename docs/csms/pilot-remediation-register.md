## Closure requirements

An action may be closed only when:
1. The accountable owner is assigned and has completed the action.
2. Objective implementation evidence is linked (approved procedure, completed records, training/briefing record, system configuration or test result as applicable).
3. An independent reviewer checks the evidence and records a decision.
4. Any residual risk or limitation is accepted by the authorized management role.
5. The status, closure date and evidence references are recorded.

## Pilot re-entry gate

A real customer pilot remains **not authorized** until management documents approval of the service model, verified principal criteria, legal/claims review, competent personnel, impartiality safeguards, methodology, data governance, commercial terms and report authorization. The ten actions above are necessary remediation items but do not replace those broader pre-launch approvals.

## RA-05 — Competence and personnel authorization evidence criteria

**Current status: OPEN.** The competence matrix is a strengthened working template only. No individual has been appointed or authorized by this register, and no credential or experience claim is verified by the existence of the template.

### Required implementation evidence

1. Management-approved role descriptions, competence criteria and delegated authorization authority.
2. Named personnel register with relevant education, qualifications, sector/activity experience and task-specific evidence.
3. Independent verification record for every mandatory licence, registration, client/principal credential or scheme authorization, including issuer, validity, scope and expiry where applicable.
4. Demonstrated competence evidence (for example, observed task, reviewed work sample, supervised assignment and documented evaluator decision); course attendance alone is insufficient.
5. Engagement-specific assignment authorization mapping each person to permitted tasks, scope, supervision and restrictions.
6. Evidence that the technical reviewer is competent for the relevant scope and independent from the work reviewed.
7. Conflict screening, site induction/safety readiness where relevant, and controlled storage of personnel records.
8. Independent closure review confirming the above evidence is complete and current.

### Closure decision

RA-05 may be marked **Closed** only after actual personnel and assignment records exist, mandatory credentials have been verified with reliable issuing sources, authorization is approved by a delegated authority, and an independent reviewer records acceptance. Draft criteria, blank forms, hypothetical names, training plans or statements in chat do not satisfy closure.

## RA-06 — Impartiality and conflict-of-interest evidence criteria

**Current status: OPEN.** The framework and role controls have been strengthened, but no actual engagement declarations, threat evaluations, safeguard implementation or independent approvals are evidenced by this document update.

### Required implementation evidence

1. Management-approved impartiality policy, threat evaluation criteria, hard-stop conditions, fee restrictions and escalation authority.
2. Formal appointment and delegation for an impartiality / compliance reviewer with authority independent of sales and delivery outcomes.
3. Engagement-specific declarations from all assigned employees, management involved in the engagement, subcontractors and external experts.
4. Recorded screening of prior advisory/document-preparation/training/implementation work, personal and financial interests, ownership/employment links, referral arrangements, commercial incentives, confidentiality and competing duties.
5. Fact-based threat evaluation covering actual, potential and perceived conflicts, with rationale and residual risk.
6. Named safeguards with owners, due dates, implementation evidence and effectiveness checks.
7. Recorded role separation for advisory, assessment, technical review, impartiality review and report approval.
8. Independent proceed / restricted / pause / decline decision before work begins, plus reassessment after changes and at report approval.
9. Evidence that any unacceptable conflict, outcome-linked incentive or improper pressure resulted in rejection, stop-work or removal of the threat; client consent alone is not closure evidence.
10. Independent closure review confirming the complete evidence package.

### Closure decision

RA-06 may be marked **Closed** only after the policy and authority are formally approved, actual engagement declarations and screening are completed, safeguards are implemented and checked, and an independent authorized reviewer records the decision. A blank template, generic declaration, planned safeguard or client waiver does not close RA-06.

## RA-07 — Technical review and quality assurance evidence criteria

**Current status: OPEN.** Draft procedure, checklist, register and role-governance wording have been added as documentation controls. They do not demonstrate management approval, an appointed reviewer, a completed engagement review or an authorized report release.

### Required implementation evidence

1. Management-approved technical review procedure, reviewer competence criteria, role segregation, escalation authority, record controls and report-release delegation.
2. Named reviewer appointment(s) with defined scope, verified relevant qualifications/experience, demonstrated review competence, current task authorization, capacity and restrictions.
3. Engagement-specific conflict declaration and impartiality clearance proving the reviewer is independent of the assessment, report authorship and any conflicting advisory/implementation work.
4. At least one completed, authorized engagement technical review using the approved checklist, with the exact reviewed report ID/version, complete review inputs, criterion-by-criterion results, rationale for N/A items and reviewer sign-off.
5. Review comment/correction log with unique IDs, severity, affected sections/evidence, author response, supporting evidence, reviewer verification and closure of all release-blocking issues.
6. Technical disagreement/escalation record for any disagreement that actually arose, including independent authority competence, conflict check, evidence, decision and rationale. If none arose, document the review-specific basis for no escalation.
7. Separately authorized report approver and release record tied to the exact reviewed report version, release decision, date and controlled distribution record.
8. Controlled revision history showing changes after review and evidence of re-review where a change could affect findings, conclusions or intended use.
9. Independent closure verification confirming evidence authenticity, completeness, currentness, traceability, authorization and effectiveness.

### Closure decision

RA-07 may be marked **Closed** only after the required procedure and delegations are formally approved, reviewer competence and independence are verified for the relevant scope, at least one real authorized engagement has a complete accepted review record, all release-blocking comments are closed, and report release is authorized by a separately delegated approver. An independent closure reviewer must record acceptance.

A blank checklist, draft procedure, hypothetical simulation, reviewer name without competence/appointment evidence, unsigned report, or technical review signature without a separate release authorization does not satisfy closure. Technical review does not confer certification, principal qualification or regulatory authority.
