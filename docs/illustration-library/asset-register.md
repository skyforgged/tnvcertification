## Approval requirements for future third-party assets

Before adding a third-party asset, verify:
1. The exact asset and its source can be identified.
2. The license permits the intended commercial website use.
3. Attribution, redistribution, modification, and other restrictions are understood.
4. Required attribution is planned for the relevant page or project credits.
5. The SVG master and generated WebP are stored at the documented paths.

Record the date the license was checked, not merely the date the file was downloaded.
