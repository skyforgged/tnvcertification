# P15 — Content Expansion & Editorial Strategy

## Status and scope

P15 is a planning and prioritization phase. It establishes an editorial roadmap from the current article inventory; it does not publish new articles, activate service/scheme records, or claim certification authority.

The current content baseline contains nine articles, with most focused on quality management and ISO 9001. P14 corrected the ISO 45001 article's category and removed the forced category from a broad compliance article. This phase aims to expand topical coverage while preserving that editorial integrity.

## Editorial objectives

1. Expand beyond the existing concentration on Quality Management and ISO 9001.
2. Build useful topic clusters that answer real implementation, audit, and management questions.
3. Avoid keyword cannibalization and near-duplicate articles.
4. Connect articles to services only through explicit, validated relationships; topical similarity is not a service relationship.
5. Avoid claims about TNCV's accreditation, authorization, scope, availability, or certification decisions unless verified and approved.
6. Prioritize practical, original, reader-oriented content over thin SEO pages.

## Editorial quality checklist

Before publication, each article must:

- Answer one clearly defined search/reader intent.
- Have an original outline and avoid repeating existing article sections.
- Have an H1, exactly one intro paragraph immediately after the H1, and the intro must begin with the article title followed by “adalah ...”.
- Include a unique, relevant meta description that is not copied from the intro or featured-image alt.
- Include exactly one featured image object with concise, specific alt text that explains the title using “adalah”.
- Use a logical heading hierarchy without skipped levels.
- Use examples as illustrations, not as legal, technical, or certification guarantees.
- Include a category only if the topic has a clear fit; otherwise leave it uncategorized.
- Use explicit translation keys for Indonesian/English versions; do not translate mechanically.
- Include accurate publication/update metadata; do not fabricate dates.
- Add service relationships only when explicitly justified by an existing validated service record.
- Be reviewed for currentness, unsupported claims, readability, and internal-link usefulness.

## Content workflow

1. Select a topic and confirm it is not duplicative of the existing inventory.
2. Research official standard sources and reliable technical sources; record edition/date as relevant.
3. Create a unique outline and editorial angle before drafting.
4. Draft Indonesian content first, then produce a meaning-equivalent English version when ready.
5. Review technical accuracy and certification-related wording.
6. Validate frontmatter, headings, images, taxonomy, translation parity, internal links, and SEO metadata.
7. Run project quality checks locally; inspect the built page and mobile layout.
8. Publish only when review and validation pass.

## Deferred / excluded from P15

- Do not activate planned taxonomy categories merely because articles are drafted.
- Do not publish certification scheme records or service pages based only on the editorial roadmap.
- Do not claim accreditation, authorization, scope, availability, client results, statistics, or pricing without evidence.
- Do not generate articles at scale before validating their usefulness and overlap.
