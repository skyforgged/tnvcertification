# Service Catalog — Planned Services

## CSMS (Contractor Safety Management System)

CSMS is added to the TNCVCertification service catalog as a planned service for further verification.

- **Service name:** Contractor Safety Management System (CSMS)
- **Indonesian name:** Penilaian Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan Kontraktor
- **Slug:** `csms-contractor-safety-management-system`
- **Category:** Occupational Health & Safety / Contractor Safety
- **Status:** Planned / Pending Verification
- **Audience:** Contractors, vendors, suppliers, service providers, construction companies, oil & gas, mining, and organizations requiring contractor safety qualification.

### Intended assessment scope (subject to validation)

- Review contractor occupational health and safety (HSE/K3) documentation and policies.
- Evaluate hazard identification and risk assessment.
- Review workforce competence and relevant training.
- Evaluate safe work procedures and operational readiness.
- Assess implementation of contractor safety-management requirements.
- Review compliance with applicable requirements from the hiring/client organization.

### Relationship to other services

- **ISO 45001:** A relevant occupational health and safety management-system reference, but CSMS is not identical to ISO 45001 certification.
- **HSE assessment / contractor safety assessment:** Related assessment services that may overlap in scope.
- **K3 training:** May support contractor competence and readiness.

### Publishing and claims safeguards

CSMS must remain marked as planned/pending verification in the service catalog until the scheme, assessment methodology, criteria/scoring, scope, and any required authorization or recognition are verified.

Do not claim that PT. Intiprima Sertifikasi is authorized to issue formal CSMS certificates, provide official scoring, or has specific recognition unless evidence is verified and approved.

Distinguish this service from **Cybersecurity Management System (CSMS)** in the automotive context (e.g. UN R155); this catalog entry refers specifically to **Contractor Safety Management System**.
