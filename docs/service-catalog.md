# Service Catalog — Planned Services

## CSMS (Contractor Safety Management System)

CSMS is added to the TNCVCertification service catalog as a planned service for further verification.

- **Service name:** Contractor Safety Management System (CSMS)
- **Indonesian name:** Penilaian Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan Kontraktor
- **Slug:** `csms-contractor-safety-management-system`
- **Category:** Occupational Health & Safety / Contractor Safety
- **Status:** Planned / Pending Verification
- **Audience:** Contractors, vendors, suppliers, service providers, construction companies, oil & gas, mining, and organizations requiring contractor safety qualification.

### Intended assessment scope (subject to validation)

- Review contractor occupational health and safety (HSE/K3) documentation and policies.
- Evaluate hazard identification and risk assessment.
- Review workforce competence and relevant training.
- Evaluate safe work procedures and operational readiness.
- Assess implementation of contractor safety-management requirements.
- Review compliance with applicable requirements from the hiring/client organization.

**Benchmark priority:** High  
**Review date:** 21 November 2026  
**Competitor reference:** [Jasa Perijinan CSMS](https://jasa-perijinan-csms.vercel.app/)

The two pages below are treated as the primary competitor references for the initial CSMS service benchmark:

- [CSMS Elements](https://jasa-perijinan-csms.vercel.app/csms-elements)

#### Services observed

The competitor's service page presents four main service groups:

| Competitor service | Scope presented | Market category |
|---|---|---|
| CSMS document development from scratch | Preparation of CSMS elements, HSE/K3LL manual, procedures, and forms | Documentation consultancy |
| CSMS review and gap analysis | Document review, gap identification, and improvement recommendations | Assessment / advisory |
| HSE verification and audit assistance | Audit simulation, verification preparation, and corrective-action assistance | Implementation assistance |
| K3LL digitalization and standardization | Document digitalization, reporting templates, and system training | Digital enablement / training |

These offerings suggest a customer journey that can be organized into four needs: **preparation → evaluation → verification readiness → ongoing system management**. This is a competitor service-positioning observation, not evidence that the provider is an accredited certification body or has authority to issue a principal's CSMS qualification.

#### Nine CSMS elements presented by the competitor

The competitor's CSMS Elements page presents the following nine-element structure:

| No. | Element | Main subject |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leadership & Commitment | Management involvement and commitment |
| 2 | Policy & Strategic Objectives | HSE/K3LL policy and objectives |
| 3 | Organization & Responsibilities | Structure, roles, responsibilities, and communication |
| 4 | Risk Management | Hazard identification, risk assessment, and controls |
| 5 | Planning & Procedures | Operational planning, procedures, and safe work practices |
| 6 | Implementation & Monitoring | Implementation and monitoring of HSE/K3LL controls |
| 7 | HSE/K3LL Audit & Performance Review | Audit and review of implementation |
| 8 | HSE/K3LL Performance Achievement | Incident statistics and HSE performance |
| 9 | Other Achievements | Supporting awards, certifications, or other achievements |

**Important qualification:** this nine-element structure is the framework presented by this competitor. It must not be described as a universal, mandatory, or nationally standardized CSMS model without an authoritative source establishing that status. CSMS requirements and element structures can differ between principal/client organizations and sectors.

#### Principal-specific comparison and implications

Available principal/client references indicate that CSMS qualification is scheme- or owner-specific. For example:

- [PGN Contractor Safety Management System guidance](https://ir.pgn.co.id/AssetFiles/esg/Contractor_Safety_Management_System.pdf) describes its own CSMS approach.
- [Pertamina Hulu Energi CSMS reference document](https://phe.pertamina.com/uploads/2023/06/60e19136-9a6e-4deb-bb78-41a7d3de4af4.pdf) provides a principal-specific qualification checklist.
- [PLN Nusantara Power CSMS FAQ](https://csms.plnnusantarapower.co.id/index.php/welcome/faq) describes its own process and stages.
- [PLN Batam CSMS announcement](https://www.plnbatam.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Surat-Pengumuman-CSMS-PLN-Batam.pdf) refers to its own application and submission mechanism.

Therefore, TNCVCertification must not combine a competitor's nine elements and different principal requirements into one supposedly universal checklist. Any future assessment criteria, scoring, evidence list, or procedure must identify the applicable principal, sector, and current source document.

#### Potential TNCVCertification service opportunities

The competitor benchmark identifies demand areas that may be evaluated for TNCVCertification, subject to competence, impartiality, legal review, and validation of the actual service model:

| Potential service | Preliminary position |
|---|---|
| CSMS Documentation Development | Potential consultancy/documentation service; verify competence and boundaries |
| CSMS Documentation Review | Potential review service; define criteria and avoid implying principal approval |
| CSMS Readiness / Gap Assessment | Potential advisory assessment; identify the reference scheme and limitations |
| CSMS Implementation Assistance | Potential assistance service; establish competence and impartiality safeguards |
| Contractor HSE Performance Evaluation | Requires a defined methodology, evidence criteria, and authority review |
| Formal CSMS qualification or certificate issuance | **Do not offer or claim** unless the relevant principal explicitly authorizes or recognizes TNCVCertification for that scheme |

A safe initial positioning, if approved after validation, would focus on **CSMS readiness, documentation review, gap assessment, and implementation assistance** rather than claiming to issue a generic or principal-issued CSMS certificate.

#### Benchmark conclusions and next research actions

1. Expand the benchmark to at least 10 providers, separating consultants, training providers, assessment providers, and principal/client scheme owners.
2. Record publicly disclosed service packages, deliverables, prices, and terms where available; do not infer undisclosed prices.
3. Verify the origin and applicability of each CSMS framework, including principal-specific requirements and current revisions.
4. Validate the competency, impartiality, legal, accreditation, authorization, and recognition requirements relevant to each proposed TNCVCertification service.
5. Define service boundaries and deliverables before preparing public-facing marketing copy.
6. Keep CSMS out of the published service catalog until the service model and claims have been reviewed and approved.

### Relationship to other services

- **ISO 45001:** A relevant occupational health and safety management-system reference, but CSMS is not identical to ISO 45001 certification.
- **HSE assessment / contractor safety assessment:** Related assessment services that may overlap in scope.
- **K3 training:** May support contractor competence and readiness.

### Publishing and claims safeguards

CSMS must remain marked as planned/pending verification in the service catalog until the scheme, assessment methodology, criteria/scoring, scope, and any required authorization or recognition are verified.

Do not claim that PT. Intiprima Sertifikasi is authorized to issue formal CSMS certificates, provide official scoring, or has specific recognition unless evidence is verified and approved.

Distinguish this service from **Cybersecurity Management System (CSMS)** in the automotive context (e.g. UN R155); this catalog entry refers specifically to **Contractor Safety Management System**.
