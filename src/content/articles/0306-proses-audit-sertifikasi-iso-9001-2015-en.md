  - type: content-section
    id: 0306-tujuan-audit-sertifikasi-en
    title: Purpose of the Certification Audit
    description: The certification audit assesses whether the quality management system has been established, implemented, and maintained in line with ISO 9001:2026 requirements. Auditors collect objective evidence through review of documented information, interviews, observation of activities, and examination of relevant records. An audit is not merely a document completeness check. The assessment looks at how processes are actually run, how results are monitored, and how the organisation handles problems and improvement.
  - type: content-section
    id: 0306-audit-tahap-1-en
    title: Stage 1 Audit
    description: The stage 1 audit focuses on readiness review and an initial understanding of the quality management system. Activities may include checking the scope, documented information, site conditions, main processes, and organisational readiness for the stage 2 audit. Stage 1 results inform planning for the next audit. Any matters needing action before stage 2 are communicated to the organisation under certification procedures.
  - type: content-section
    id: 0306-audit-tahap-2-en
    title: Stage 2 Audit
    description: The stage 2 audit assesses implementation and effectiveness of the quality management system within the submitted scope. Auditors may trace processes from input to output, check operational controls, evaluate performance monitoring, and interview personnel carrying out the related activities. Audit evidence must be relevant, verifiable, and sufficient to support conclusions. Sampling is part of the audit process, so an audit does not mean checking every transaction or all organisational records.
  - type: content-section
    id: 0306-temuan-dan-tindakan-korektif-en
    title: Findings and Corrective Action
    description: Where evidence shows that a requirement is unmet, auditors record a nonconformity under the applicable procedure. The organisation is responsible for making corrections, analysing causes, defining corrective action, and providing evidence of implementation and effectiveness within the agreed time limit. Corrective action needs to address causes so that similar nonconformities do not recur. The form of verification and evidence required depends on the nature of the finding and certification process rules.
  - type: content-section
    id: 0306-tinjauan-dan-keputusan-sertifikasi-en
    title: Review and Certification Decision
    description: Audit results do not automatically mean a certificate is issued. Before a decision is made, audit results and evidence of nonconformity closure are reviewed by authorised personnel independent of the audit under the certification body's mechanism. The certification decision considers all evidence and applicable scheme rules. If requirements remain unmet, the decision and follow-up are communicated under certification procedures.
  - type: content-section
    id: 0306-sikap-organisasi-selama-audit-en
    title: Organisational Conduct During the Audit
    description: The organisation needs to make sure relevant personnel understand the process and their responsibilities, relevant information is available, and audit access is arranged as planned. Answers should be factual and supported by implementation evidence, not merely statements that procedures exist. An effective audit is professional, objective, and focused on evidence. Its results describe system conformity within the scope and period assessed.
  - type: content-section
    id: 0306-catatan-edisi-standar-en
    title: Standard Edition Note
    description: ISO 9001:2026 was published on 16 September 2026 as the sixth edition, replacing the 2015 edition. Organisations holding ISO 9001:2015 certificates need to confirm transition plans and rules with their certification body. ISO/TC 176/SC 2 guidance names 30 September 2029 as the global transition deadline; operational schedules still follow accreditation body rules and the relevant scope. This article gives a general explanation of certification audits and does not promise a particular audit result or certification decision.
