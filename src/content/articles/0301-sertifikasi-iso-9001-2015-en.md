  - type: content-section
    id: 0301-mengenal-iso-9001-2026-en
    title: Understanding ISO 9001:2026
    description: ISO 9001 sets out quality management system requirements that organisations of different sizes and sectors can apply. The 2026 edition is the sixth edition and replaces ISO 9001:2015 as the current edition. Its focus remains the organisation's ability to provide products and services consistently, meet customer requirements and applicable statutory requirements, and improve quality management system performance. The standard does not prescribe one organisational structure or one document set for every company. System arrangements need to reflect the organisation's context, processes, risks, and needs.
  - type: content-section
    id: 0301-ruang-lingkup-sistem-manajemen-mutu-en
    title: Scope of the Quality Management System
    description: Before certification, an organisation needs to define the scope of its quality management system clearly. The scope identifies the products or services, locations, functions, and processes included in the system being assessed. The organisation also needs to understand its main and supporting processes, their interactions, responsibilities, and how quality objectives are monitored. Documented information is used as needed to support system effectiveness and provide evidence of implementation.
  - type: content-section
    id: 0301-gambaran-tahapan-sertifikasi-en
    title: Overview of the Certification Stages
    description: "In general, quality management system certification includes the stages below. Stage details, timing, and administrative requirements may differ according to scope, scheme, and the rules of the authorised certification body."
    items:
      - title: Application and scope definition
        description: the organisation submits basic information about the entity, locations, activities, and requested scope.
      - title: Audit planning
        description: the certification body reviews relevant information and sets an audit plan suited to the scope and complexity of the organisation.
      - title: Certification audit
        description: the audit follows the stages set out in the certification programme, including document review and assessment of system implementation at the relevant location or process.
      - title: Closure of findings
        description: where nonconformities are identified, the organisation makes corrections and takes corrective action, then submits the required evidence.
      - title: Review and certification decision
        description: a decision is made through an independent review based on audit results and certification scheme rules.
      - title: Surveillance and recertification
        description: after a certificate is issued, continued certification follows the applicable surveillance and recertification programme.
  - type: content-section
    id: 0301-peralihan-dari-iso-9001-2015-en
    title: Transition from ISO 9001:2015
    description: Organisations already certified to ISO 9001:2015 need to plan migration to the 2026 edition. ISO/TC 176/SC 2 transition guidance sets 30 September 2029 as the global deadline for transitioning 2015-edition certificates. Transition audit arrangements and schedules still need confirmation with the relevant certification body, because implementation follows accreditation body rules and the relevant recognition scope. Organisations should check requirement changes in the official standard copy, assess the effect on the current system, and prepare a transition plan based on applicable official information.
  - type: content-section
    id: 0301-hal-yang-perlu-diperhatikan-en
    title: Points to Consider
    description: Certification does not replace the organisation's responsibility to control processes and meet customer and regulatory requirements. A certificate is not a guarantee that every product or service is always free from nonconformity. The value of a quality management system lies in consistent application, evidence-based evaluation, and effective follow-up.
