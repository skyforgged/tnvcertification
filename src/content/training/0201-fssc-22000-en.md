  - type: content-section
    id: "audit-evidence-techniques-en"
    layout: "editorial"
    eyebrow: "Auditor skills"
    title: "Techniques for collecting and evaluating audit evidence"
    description: "Audit conclusions must rest on relevant, verifiable information. Interview answers or the existence of documents alone are not enough; information needs to be traced and compared with audit criteria."
    items:
      - title: "Personnel interviews"
        description: "Questions are open, clear, and matched to the process under examination. Auditors can ask about responsibilities, work methods, controls, and records that demonstrate process performance."
      - title: "Process observation"
        description: "Observation establishes actual conditions in the work area and compares them with defined procedures or controls. Auditors record visible facts, not assumptions about causes."
      - title: "Document and record review"
        description: "Documents show defined arrangements; records can provide evidence of performance. Auditors check relevance, consistency, and connection with the audited process."
      - title: "Tracing and sampling"
        description: "Auditors trace information or process flow and select samples relevant to audit objectives. Samples must be sufficient to support conclusions within the examination scope."
      - title: "Information triangulation"
        description: "Information from interviews, observation, and documents is compared so conclusions are not drawn from one unverified source."

        description: "The finding describes the observed fact, the related location or process where relevant, supporting evidence, and the requirement reference. Wording stays factual and evidence-based."

        description: "Findings are discussed with relevant parties so facts are understood accurately. Differences of view are handled by returning to the evidence and audit criteria."
      - title: "Preparing the report"

    title: "Six stages of an FSSC 22000 internal audit"
    steps:
      - title: "Establishing the audit programme"
