      - question: "Is CSMS the same as ISO 45001 certification?"
        answer: "No. ISO 45001 is an occupational health and safety management system standard, while CSMS follows criteria set by an employer or project owner. The two can complement each other, but neither replaces the other."
      - question: "Who sets CSMS requirements?"
        answer: "Requirements are usually set by the employer, project owner, or scheme used during prequalification and project execution."

        answer: "No. The review also checks whether safety controls are applied in the field and whether findings receive timely follow-up."
